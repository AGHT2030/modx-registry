## Public Framework Statement

MODX is a sovereign digital infrastructure framework designed to merge human-world activity with AI/digital-world execution through governed bridges, oracle validation, and event-routed movement.

The MODX movement architecture is organized around three coordinated layers:

1. **MODLINK** — the bridge and interoperability fabric.
2. **Oracles** — the validation, truth, and interpretation layer.
3. **ERGO** — the governed movement and routing intelligence layer.

Together, MODLINK, Oracles, and ERGO form the MODX Holy Trinity of Parallel Movement.

MODLINK connects available pathways between MODX environments, applications, wallets, marketplaces, sectors, planets, and external systems. Oracles confirm whether a signal, identity, asset, event, or route is valid. ERGO carries verified movement across the selected MODLINK bridge or wormhole and releases the appropriate output into the user-facing MODX universe.

MODX uses a parallel-universe infrastructure model. In this model, human-world inputs such as identity, capital, assets, creator activity, infrastructure demand, hospitality activity, franchise activity, and marketplace behavior are compressed, validated, routed, transformed, and released as verified digital outputs.

The MODX visual galaxy represents this structure through planets, rings, bridges, wormholes, border modules, event pulses, and output bursts. Planet rings may represent growth, liquidity, bonds, ETFs, participation, or marketplace maturity. Movement pulses are intended to be event-gated and should occur only when verified events are confirmed by the system.

## Architectural Metaphor

The terms black hole, wormhole, bridge, parallel universe, and white hole are used as digital infrastructure metaphors:

- **Black-hole layer**: intake, compression, and normalization of human-world inputs.
- **Wormhole / bridge layer**: governed MODLINK routes between environments.
- **Oracle layer**: validation and interpretation of movement eligibility.
- **ERGO layer**: event-routed movement across verified routes.
- **White-hole output layer**: verified release of value, intelligence, rewards, growth, or market outputs.

These terms describe software architecture, interface representation, and event-routing behavior. They do not claim physical creation of astrophysical phenomena.
